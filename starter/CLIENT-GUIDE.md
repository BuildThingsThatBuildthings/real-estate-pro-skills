# Put AI Acceleration in your AI

Start with the AI you already use. Copy the installation prompt from START-HERE.html or INSTALL-PROMPT.md and give it to your agent. If it cannot fetch this release from GitHub, attach the downloaded starter package.

## What you get

- **My Context Card:** your identity, voice, business knowledge and preferences.
- **Content:** useful posts, emails and production briefs based on your real source material.
- **Listing Machine:** listing context, descriptions and launch materials.
- **Listing Photos:** image review, staging directions and image editing when your AI has an available image tool.

The installer adds four skills. Your existing AI does the reasoning and creation. MCP is not required for this release, and context/output files do not automatically synchronize with the AIA portal.

## What your agent will ask

Your business, market, voice and first useful task. Then the email, calendar, CRM, files and communications relevant to that task. You can skip a connection or use the fictional practice listing. Reuse existing context instead of starting over.

Sign into providers through their own account screens. Never paste passwords or keys into a chat. The agent verifies the exact account and performs a small read you select. Sending messages, creating meetings, changing CRM records and publishing need your explicit direction.

## Installation depends on your AI

**An agent with workspace access:** use the prompt to inspect and run the included starter installer. It supports a selected Claude or Codex workspace, preserves conflicting files and verifies the copied files. Refresh the host's skill discovery if needed, then invoke a skill to prove it loaded.

**An AI app with skill uploads:** upload the individual skill ZIPs through the app's supported skill settings. The agent should guide you using the current interface. Do not assume every account or plan has that feature.

**An AI app that only accepts chat attachments:** attach the relevant SKILL.md and your context file. You can run the method in that conversation, but this is session use rather than a persistent installation.

## Your first result

Ask: “Use the fictional Cedar House practice listing to draft a description and launch email in my voice. Show the unanswered questions separately.”

A good result uses only the supplied facts, labels the example fictional, leaves price and size unknown, and gives you editable work. Review it before using any method with real client material.

For photos, begin with an original image you are allowed to use. Your AI checks whether it has an image tool. If not, you receive a staging brief, not a claim that an image was generated.

## Keep the setup

Save context.md and business-connections.md in the workspace you chose. Keep business and client information out of public repositories. Keep originals and review notes with generated work.

Your setup receipt should distinguish: files installed; skill loaded by the AI; context saved; connections verified; and first useful result. These are separate milestones. If something fails, keep the result you have and resolve that specific step.

## Help

Use the help contact shown in your AIA portal or ask in the AI Acceleration Skool community. Describe your AI app, the step that failed and the error without including passwords, tokens or private client information.
