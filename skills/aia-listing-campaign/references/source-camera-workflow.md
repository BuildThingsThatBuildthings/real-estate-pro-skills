# Photograph-to-camera footage

## Bundled capability readiness and source intake

This standard full campaign uses only the installed AIA bundle and its already callable standard capabilities. Do not require optional paid providers, new purchases, third-party accounts, credits or sign-ins. Kling is excluded from the current rebuild; an earlier sign-in authorization does not override the user's later rejection. Do not present an optional provider as a mandatory solution or treat an existing subscription as permission to use a withdrawn route.

Inspect supplied original camera footage first. The user-authorized basic-editor route uses original property photographs with bundled FFmpeg/Pillow: controlled framing and motion, varied shot timing, clean cuts, narration, music and a coherent full-home narrative. It does not require a paid generation service. These are edited source photographs, not a measured 3D scan or invented camera footage. Review the actual full film for engaging pacing, meaningful coverage and visual payoff. Do not substitute repeated title panels, padded long holds or a short teaser for the requested flagship. Source-preserving motion cannot reveal architecture absent from the photographs.

Normalize incompatible source formats with the bundled source-image preparation script, retaining originals and hashes. Every successful media operation must return actual usable media and genuine execution evidence. Never describe a prompt, plan or mock result as produced footage.

Save newly generated accepted clips in this run's private footage workspace. In each editor shot, bind the accepted bytes to `source_sha256`, `origin: generated`, `origin_run_id`, source photograph IDs, provider job provenance and review evidence. Original captured footage uses `origin: original` with original-source rights; it must not be a generated clip relabeled as camera footage. The upstream reset/lineage gate must establish that distinction. Derivative clips inherit their original generated run; re-encoding never resets provenance. Earlier generated clips are excluded from a user-directed fresh rebuild regardless of quality.

Keep the photographed home as the reference. Decide exactly what the shot must reveal and which photographs establish that space. Describe one camera path, foreground object, final composition and bounded travel. Use a wider corroborating angle before asking the model to expose hidden architecture.

Inspect the provider's current media-role support. An exact first-frame mode may accept one image while a multi-reference mode accepts several without preserving the opening frame. Never promise both unless the selected mode actually supports both. When switching modes, record the tradeoff and compare the generated first frame against all source photographs.

Generate one test before building a batch. Save provider, model, job, prompt, inputs, quoted allocation, returned file and source-comparison evidence. Status is `generated_unreviewed` until the actual video is checked. A provider assistant announcing a generation is not a submitted job: verify a job tile or identifier, completion state and downloaded media.

Review all footage for disappearing furniture, invented tables, extra windows, moved doors, shifting ceiling lights, stretched edges and false connections. A smooth camera path alone does not pass. Compare the ending to a real reverse-angle photo when available. Reject a bad segment; keep its failure reason privately. An explicit user reset deletes failed takes as well as previously accepted generations. A short valid portion of a current-run take may be used only after checking that portion independently.

Separate shot generation from editing. Build a feature reveal with a coordinated visual, spoken and written opening; let the actual rooms fill the frame. Edit around buyer-life moments and sound, not identical durations or repeated title panels. Do not reuse the same eight-second move at half speed merely to satisfy a requested duration.

A licensing or source-coverage gap remains a disclosed production dependency. The authorized photo-edit route can produce a finished property story, but its quality must be judged from the actual film and its photographic nature represented honestly. Do not add a paid provider to remove that gap in this standard version.
