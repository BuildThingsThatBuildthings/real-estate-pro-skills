# Source-preserving motion and multimedia editing

## Explicit basic video-editing route

When the user explicitly requests a basic edit of original listing photographs using the standard AIA bundle, use `edit_camera_sequence.py` with `mode: basic_photo_edit`. This is an authored photographic film with editorial movement, not a measured 3D scan or a physically captured walkthrough. This explicit instruction changes the source medium; it is never a hidden fallback for a still-required camera-footage commission. No paid provider, add-on or account is needed.

Author every shot from an inspected original photograph, with `origin: original`, source identity/hash, `status: reviewed`, short `seconds` and `motion: {start:{x,y,width,height},end:{x,y,width,height}}` in original pixels. Both framing windows must remain inside the actual source and match output aspect. Choose the direction from the photograph: lateral reveal, gentle push, pullback or vertical detail reveal. Vary the direction, scale and shot duration across the story; never repeat a universal zoom preset or use an invented room portal. The renderer samples these explicit windows with bicubic interpolation at the output dimensions. It does not invent parallax geometry, hidden walls or neighbors.

For a75–90second full-home flagship, use at least28distinct useful original photographs, generally2–4seconds per picture, and exact story-led cuts. Prioritize entry, work, connected living, primary suite, upper-floor flexibility, practical detail, patio and home-in-place; avoid redundant bathroom angles simply to hit count. Lead with the strongest authored property reveal and return to a memorable home/patio image. Narration, readable embedded captions, soundtrack and actual complete playback remain required. Retain separate portrait compositions with independently chosen crop windows. Full-frame photography dominates; no boxed presentation panels or misleading fabricated continuity.

## Executable film route

When actual original panoramic tour photographs are available, apply [original panoramas](original-panoramas.md) before settling on ordinary photo framing. Use `mode: panorama_photo_edit` for a mixed film. Every panorama clip retains its generated origin and same-run producer, plus `panorama_config` and the exactly matching reviewed `room_identity`. Declare and consume the parent config and original panorama as inputs alongside the rendered clip. The editor checks original hashes, parent output identity and rights. A flagship must contain at least five distinct panorama capture positions across four actual spaces, with panoramic movement carrying at least35percent of its running time, plus eight distinct source photographs and15purposeful shots. These are coverage floors, not an instruction to pad footage. Preserve narrative order and actual connections. Virtual rotation within a panoramic photograph must never be described as physical camera travel or a measured3Dreconstruction.

Use `scripts/edit_camera_sequence.py` for finished cinematic films. `prepare_films.py RUN` now validates explicit `context/film-timelines.json` compositions and writes only private working configs; it no longer invokes a photo renderer. Do not call the retired photo-props path or `final_mix.py` to produce new camera films. Each horizontal and vertical composition must have its own authored shot timeline, not resized copies of another composition.

The private timeline contains `run_id`, `composition_id`, `kind` (`flagship`, `launch`, `neighborhood`, `reel`, `teaser`), `working`, MP4 `output`, `width`, `height`, `fps`, `shots`, `music`, and, for full films, `narration` and `captions`. Each shot provides video `path`, source `in` and `seconds`, `status: reviewed`, `origin: original|generated`, reviewed `source_sha256`, and `origin_run_id` for generated footage. The generated run must match the timeline. A source with a different aspect requires an explicit in-bounds `crop` rectangle (`x`, `y`, `width`, `height`) matching the composition; no implicit letterbox or destructive automatic crop is available. Prior-run generated media, still-image inputs, overlapping reused source intervals, speed stretching and insufficient shot duration fail.

## Audio is a separate block, never baked into a picture render

Binding (Ryan, 2026-09-18): "the narration isn't even supposed to be built into the video, you need to create the video and then the audio track... that is a bullshit ai short cut." A render that muxes its own audio inline is a failed build, because changing the voice then costs a full re-render and no editor can be handed stems.

Build and deliver in layers:

- `picture/{composition_id}.mp4` — SILENT picture master (`-an`). Final motion, type and timing.
- `audio/music/{composition_id}.wav` and `audio/vo/{composition_id}.wav` — full-length stems, sample-accurate, aligned to frame 0 of the picture master.
- `audio/mix/{composition_id}.json` — the mix as DATA (per-stem gain, duck envelope, fades, offsets), not logic buried in the renderer.
- A separate mix/mux step produces the delivered MP4 and MUST use `-c:v copy`; it never re-encodes picture.
- Delivery includes the mixed MP4 **plus** the silent picture master **plus** the stems.

The acceptance test: dropping a real human voiceover into `audio/vo/` and re-mixing must cost seconds. If it would cost a re-render, the build is wrong. Cut picture to the music grid, never to speech — a VO is laid over afterwards and must not drive the edit.

**No synthetic/TTS voice ships in delivered audio.** Kokoro and equivalent local or hosted TTS are for internal timing scratch only, never delivery. When no licensed human voice exists, deliver a music-only mix, keep `audio/vo/` empty with a short README naming what belongs there, and pair it with the agent recording kit so the client's own recorded voice drops straight in. Because the delivered mix has no voice, on-screen type must carry every load-bearing claim.

Sound inputs contain `path` and `rights_basis`; music additionally has `attribution` and an optional source `in`. SFX additionally provide timeline `at`, `seconds`, and `gain`. Narration must fit the actual shot sequence without truncation. Music must already cover that sequence. Embedded captions use `{start,end,text}` and the chosen `font`; split copy into short readable cues and review frame boundaries. Shot text and `brand_text` accept `{text,size,x,y}`. Camera source audio is excluded; only reviewed sound inputs enter the mix. This editor uses exact cuts, fixed speech/music levels and restrained fades. Author soundtrack changes upstream when the story needs them; do not claim dynamic ducking or complex transitions it does not implement.

For local licensed narration, `context/local-narration.json` requires a fresh `run_id` and can supply `narrations: [{composition_id, segments: [{text, pause_after}]}]`. This generates independent WAVs and private sentence-timing information without stretching speech to old scene durations. Author camera durations and readable caption cues from that narration. Model, voice and settings participate in cache identity; unproven existing caches fail.

Video folders contain final MP4s and cover images only. Working configs, generated speech segments, captions, receipts and logs remain local outside delivery. Useful requested recording scripts and copy remain separate document deliverables; do not export technical sidecars into their folders either. The editor records consumed source paths/hashes and actual render status internally. Successful execution never marks full playback or property fidelity approved. A claimed fresh origin is only as reliable as the upstream asset lineage: hashes alone cannot detect a relabeled or re-encoded prior generation.

For workflow execution, bind every read source (shots, sound, font and CLI config) to the runner's task inputs. Supply identity fields on source objects or `input_provenance: [{path,origin,source_id,run_id,producer_task}]`; original sources require `source_id`, generated sources require their actual producing run/task. The private receipt at `working/composition_id/render-receipt.json` reports actual consumed byte hashes. Missing provenance is not auto-filled as original; the workflow gate rejects it. A config authored in the current workflow carries that author's generation provenance too.

### Agent authoring and provider operations

Use `scripts/external_operation.py begin SPEC RECORD` before agent-authored scripts/timelines or browser provider actions. The private spec includes current `run_id`, `task_id`, governing installed `skill`, `operation` (`agent_authoring` or `provider_generation`), `root`, actual `inputs` with runner identities/hashes, and absent expected `outputs`. Write the record under `working/`. Begin binds source bytes and installed skill/verifier revisions; never begin retroactively around old generated content.

After performing the AIA operation, save its actual tool response/transcript or job screenshot privately and call `external_operation.py complete RECORD EVIDENCE`. Evidence identifies the same run/task, tool/action ID, recorded time, source hashes and output hashes, concrete evidence-file paths and explicit reviewer inspection. Provider footage also requires a submitted job ID and ordered submission/completion times. The result is `externally_verified`, not a claim that a local script generated it. The runner's external task references this record; subsequent generated inputs name this producer. These checks bind inspected evidence and bytes but cannot cryptographically prove an external service executed a job. No invented IDs or manually promoted status fields.

The practical chain is: fresh AIA authoring of narrative/narration inputs and shot plans; fresh provider footage; licensed independent narration; AIA authoring of separate picture/caption timelines against that actual audio; direct camera editor execution. `prepare_films.py` is a receipt-producing validation/preparation operation if used, not a substitute for that creative authoring. Original photos/models/fonts remain originals; all new authored configs, narration and camera clips retain current-run generated producer identities. Complete sources/outputs exist privately; only the requested finished media/documents enter delivery.

Inventory original image dimensions and hashes. Inspect the actual photos, assign room/location IDs and rights. Property authorization covers only the actual authorized assets; public availability alone does not authorize third-party destination photography. Use original maps/graphics where imagery is unavailable and accurately label them; do not call these local footage.

For cinematic property footage, apply [source camera workflow](source-camera-workflow.md). Use an appropriate image-to-video model or verified captured footage, with source photographs and room topology controlling the camera treatment. A bounded photo pan can be an occasional supporting insert; it does not satisfy a requested cinematic walkthrough. Test a demanding shot before scaling. Record model, parameters, cost, job ID, source hash and actual output review. Reject geometry drift, invented neighbors, room connections and watermarks. If the chosen route fails, investigate a better supported route or surface the footage dependency; never silently replace the requested medium with photo motion.

Let real rooms dominate the frame. Use concise deterministic typography and actual brand marks. Large editorial bands and inset photographs are optional graphic treatments, not the default architecture for property films. Preserve portrait context by planning the camera and composition for that format; do not hide the room inside a presentation layout or apply a destructive automatic crop. Compose landscape and portrait independently. Use Remotion for authored graphic sequences or FFmpeg for exact cuts, audio mixing and minimal overlays on finished camera footage. Keep edit decisions in private data consumed by reusable scripts. Export h264/yuv420p, retain the native resolution honestly, and do not label a 720p generation as native 1080p.

Narration must be an authorized agent recording or commercially licensed provider voice or appropriately licensed local model. Never impersonate the agent. The narrator may invite viewers to contact the agent in third person; first-person "I'm [agent]" requires actual agent speech. Keep voice provenance in the handoff. Source music and SFX with documented permitted use. Avoid music chosen only because it was used previously. Duck music below speech, avoid clipped peaks, allow narrative pauses and deliberate ending. Do not describe library SFX as location-recorded ambience.

Teasers may run 8–12 seconds when the approved campaign specifies teaser mode; this does not change the 25-second weekly-video minimum. Different hooks should be actual rendered opening variants for the chosen films, with paired captions. Full films must fulfill requested duration and narrative rather than padding repeats to meet a timer.

## Commercial audio preflight

Before any voice/music generation, read the authenticated account plan and current provider terms. A public stock voice is not automatically commercially licensed on every plan. Free-plan internal-evaluation rights do not authorize client delivery. Record plan, terms URL/date, commercial-use status and balance. Test one short job; a successful model lookup is not proof of funded generation. Stop on terminal balance errors, without retry loops or hidden top-ups. If no licensed narration is available, deliver explicitly labeled music-only visual review cuts and the exact recording script, leaving required narrated films incomplete. Strip rejected evaluation audio from every delivered file. Licensed music with attribution can supply the visual review mix.

### Inspect the actual composition before rendering

Before starting full-film rendering, run `scripts/inspect_composition_frames.py CONFIG PRIVATE_WORKING_JPG` for every orientation. Inspect the start, midpoint and endpoint crop of every original-photo shot against its spoken claim. An uncropped source contact sheet alone cannot prove the portrait film shows the playground, door, seating or other feature named in narration. The original location may be correct while the crop hides the relevant feature. Correct the authored window before multiplying renders. This private contact sheet is a preflight aid, never full sound/muted playback evidence, and must not enter Drive delivery.
