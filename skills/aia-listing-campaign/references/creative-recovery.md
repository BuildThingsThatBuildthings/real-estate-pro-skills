# Recover a rejected creative campaign

When the user rejects the creative, stop batch production and inspect the exact outputs. Write the failure in observable terms: image area, shot duration, repeated layout, generic line, missing spatial information, mismatched voice or unearned promise. Preserve the rejected version. A passing technical gate is not an answer to a taste or effectiveness failure.

Treat the requested medium as an outcome requirement. Cinematic walkthroughs need spatially informative movement and an edit that reveals the real property. Presentation panels, arbitrary photo pans and amenity title cards are not interchangeable substitutes. If the necessary service or footage is unavailable, name the production gap; do not silently redefine the requested film.

Choose one short motion proof that exercises the hard part before multiplying formats. Judge opening impact, photographed space, spatial credibility, pacing, voice and sound. Inspect the moving result, not only a storyboard or cover. Record unresolved limitations. Do not call a whole campaign improved because its first frame looks better.

Use the agent's own posts or speech to study delivery. Separate first-person evidence, operator-written website copy, broker disclosure and inferred style. A website's legal or FAQ prose does not automatically define the agent's social personality. Keep an inference labeled; never turn an inferred rule into a prohibition that sterilizes the requested work.

Write buyer-life moments tied to visible features: where a school project might spread out, how a door separates a work call, where a movie can happen while dinner continues downstairs. These are scenarios, not claims about the current occupants or demographic eligibility. The intended viewers can picture their own household.

For spatial reconstruction, distinguish a measured scan, multi-view reconstruction, single-view depth surface and generative camera shot. Preserve source identity and label approximation where material. Do not invent hidden topology, add rooms or connect unrelated photographs. Reject stretched edges, sliding fixtures, geometry drift and artificial room transitions.

A creative review records observations and changes, not self-awarded scores or the word “locked.” The output count is a fulfillment check after the film earns expansion.

When the user identifies stronger earlier work, inspect its production files and finished evidence. Recover transferable camera, continuity and review practices; do not import its brand or fictional scenery. A proven image-to-video workflow may be more appropriate than a new presentation renderer. Verify present provider access independently: API prepayment, connector credits and a browser subscription can be separate allowances. Use existing authorized access; never purchase, change plans, evade limits or accept a new account agreement to conceal a production blocker.

For a source-led explanatory dollhouse, use [spatial diagrams](spatial-diagrams.md). For generative footage, apply [source camera workflow](source-camera-workflow.md). `generate_camera_shot.py` submits one authorized source-image shot through Gemini's documented Interactions API and records a generated-unreviewed receipt. Read current official model documentation before use. It does not grant rights, guarantee architecture fidelity, or certify the footage. Provider failure must remain a failure, not trigger a silent downgrade to photo pans.
