# Channel

How each place behaves. Edit the account list at the bottom first — the machine routes with it.

## Universal
- First line is a standalone hook, under 125 characters.
- At least one verifiable specific in every piece.
- No hashtag piles. Semantic keywords in the prose instead.
- No engagement bait ("comment YES below").
- One idea per piece.

## Video, all channels
- 25 to 75 seconds. Cap 90. Under 25 reads as a fragment.
- First frame is the point: the stat, the question, or the promise.
  Never a logo. Never a screenshot of an app.
- Mute-safe. If it only works with sound on, it does not work.
- Recipe or steps on screen as text, not just narration.

## Instagram
Caption under 220 characters. Hook in line one. Vertical 9:16, filmed
native, never cropped from horizontal.

## Facebook
200 to 400 characters. Ends with a soft question.

## LinkedIn
600 to 900 characters. Three short paragraphs. No outbound link in the
body.

## Email
Subject 35 to 50 characters, specific rather than clever. Preheader 60
to 90 characters that completes the subject rather than repeating it.
Body 80 to 180 words. One CTA, one link. A P.S. that adds a human
detail, not a second pitch.
