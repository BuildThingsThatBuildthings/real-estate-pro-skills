## Do not say
Start with 15-20. This single list is the highest-leverage thing in the
card — it cuts editing time roughly in half, because most of what you
would have fixed is the same handful of phrases every time.

Real estate filler:
- dream home
- hidden gem
- must-see
- nestled
- charming (as filler)
- cozy (when the honest word is "small")
- rustic (when the honest word is "needs work")
- boasts
- turnkey (unless it is literally move-in ready and provable)
- won't last / act now / don't miss out
