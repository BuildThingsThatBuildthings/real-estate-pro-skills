# How these folders work

Four folders. You only ever touch two of them.

## 00 – Weekly Context  ← you put things in here

This is the only folder you have to feed. Drop in anything from your week:

- A new listing, or one that just sold
- A market report or MLS stat sheet
- **Questions clients actually asked you** ← the most valuable thing you can put here
- Photos you own outright
- Anything you want talked about: a neighborhood, a process, a misconception

Don't tidy it. A screenshot of a text message is a perfectly good input. Voice memos are fine. There is no format, and there is no naming convention. If the file is called `IMG_4471.HEIC`, leave it.

**Why questions matter most:** your whole voice is answering a real question precisely. "How long does closing take?" produces better content than "post something about closing," because the first one has a real person behind it, a real misunderstanding to correct, and a reason for a stranger to care.

## 01 – Waiting  ← you look here

Finished work, ready for you. Each week's set arrives together with a short summary of what it is and why it was made.

Three things you can do with anything in here:

1. **Approve it** — drag it to `02 – Approved`
2. **Ask for changes** — drag it to `03 – Revision Requested` and say what's wrong
3. **Leave it** — nothing expires, nothing publishes on its own

"I don't like it" is a complete answer. You don't owe anyone a reason. A reason helps the next set be better, but it is never required.

## 02 – Approved  ← you drag things here

What you've said yes to. Moving something here means it's cleared. It does not publish it.

## 03 – Revision Requested

What you've sent back. Add a note if you want, or just move it. An unexplained move is still useful signal.

## What this machine will never do

- **Publish or schedule anything by itself.** It makes things and proposes times. You decide what actually goes out.
- **Say a neighborhood is safe, or that schools are good.** Those phrases create Fair Housing problems. The machine will not write them.
- **Promise anything** — a price, a timeline, an outcome, appreciation, rent, or investment performance.
- **Use photos it isn't sure you own.** If the rights are unclear, the asset doesn't get made. Unclear rights are missing, not pending.
- **Put you on camera.** Everything here is faceless unless you decide otherwise.

## The rhythm

**You:** drop things into `00 – Weekly Context` whenever they happen.
**The machine:** builds a set each week into `01 – Waiting`.
**You:** approve, send back, or ignore.

That's it. About twenty minutes on your side, most weeks.
